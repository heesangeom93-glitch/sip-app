Sip Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Sip does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What Sip stores

Your progress, test results and word lists are stored only on your device. Sip never sends this information anywhere.

What Sip does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No third-party services
  • No network requests

Deleting your data

When you delete Sip from your iPhone, all of its data is deleted with it.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, email richcollin3000@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
